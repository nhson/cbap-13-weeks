## What went well
- Store observation sessions uncovered the staging-area and aisle-data problems early.
- The stock-confidence indicator idea was well received by IT and stores.

## What didn't
- Workshops were scheduled on Fridays, the stores' busiest delivery day.
- Requirements written by junior BAs were often compound or vague.
- The approval path for store-related requirements was unclear: Marcus or store managers?
- Marketing felt "shut down" in workshop #1.

## Ideas
- Checklist-based peer review before QA review
- Move store sessions to Tuesday/Wednesday mornings
- Clarify decision rights in the governance approach
