# Store Operations: How a Customer "Phone Order" Works Today
*Source: Store Operations manual, section 7.4 (last updated 2019), plus handwritten margin notes from Riverton Central*

> Several stores informally accept phone orders from elderly customers and local businesses (≈ 40–80 orders/week chain-wide). There is no system support.

## Official process (manual 7.4)
1. The customer calls the store's customer service desk.
2. The desk associate writes the order on form SO-12 (paper, in triplicate).
3. The order is placed in the "orders tray" and picked by the **next available associate** before 5 pm.
4. Chilled and frozen items are picked **last** and stored in the back-room cooler.
5. The customer arrives, and the desk associate retrieves the order and rings it through the POS at the desk till.
6. Payment is taken at pickup (card or cash).
7. The white copy of SO-12 is filed for 30 days.

## Margin notes (Rosa Delgado)
- *"Step 3 never happens before 5. Usually the night shift picks it."*
- *"We ring it up on the main tills, not the desk till. The desk till is broken half the time."*
- *"Substitutions: we call the customer if we can. Mrs. Olsen says 'use your judgement'."*
- *"Frozen goes straight to the ice-cream freezer at the front. The back cooler is full of flowers on Fridays."*
- *"No alcohol on phone orders. Head office rule, but nobody wrote it down."*
