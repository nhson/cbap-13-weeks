| Capability | FreshWay | QuickCart | Best-in-class (national) | GreenBasket |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Order channel | App + web | App + web + phone | App + web + voice | none |
| Pickup lead time | 2 h | 4 h | 1 h | — |
| Delivery | Yes (fee $7.99) | No | Yes | — |
| Pickup fee | Free over $50 | $2.99 | Free over $35 | — |
| Substitution preferences | Per order ("allow / don't allow") | Per item | Per item + chat with picker | — |
| Real-time store stock | "Low stock" indicator | No | Yes | — |
| Loyalty points online | Yes | Yes | Yes | — |
| Alcohol in online orders | Yes (ID at pickup) | No | Yes | — |
| Order accuracy (claimed) | 96% | not published | 99% | — |
| Edit order after checkout | Until 2 h before slot | No | Until picking starts | — |

**Lily's note:** "We should leapfrog: recipes, meal plans, AI personalized offers. Let's be the Netflix of groceries!"
