# Pilot Report: 6 stores, 10 weeks after go-live (solution: OmniGrocer Suite)
*Prepared by Tom Okafor. Data in `15-pilot-metrics.csv`.*

## Targets (from the approved business case)
| Measure | Target | Pilot average |
|---|---|---|
| Orders per store per week | 150 by week 10 | 96 |
| Order accuracy (items correct, no unapproved substitution) | ≥ 97% | 93.1% |
| Ready within 2 h of order | ≥ 95% | 88% |
| Pickup wait < 5 min | ≥ 90% | 91% |
| Pick time per 30-item order | ≤ 15 min | 19 min |
| Customer satisfaction (post-pickup CSAT) | ≥ 4.3/5 | 4.4/5 |
| Online share of pilot-store sales | 2% by week 10 | 1.4% |

## Observations
- Two stores (Riverton Central, Oakfield) hit nearly every target. Two (Bayview, Hillcrest) are well below.
- Most accuracy errors are **items shown as "in stock" that were not on the shelf**. The 15-minute stock sync works, but StockPoint itself is wrong for fresh items.
- Bayview and Hillcrest managers **reassign pickers to tills** during peaks. Picking counts against store labour budgets, so managers see it as a cost.
- Hillcrest pick paths are poor because aisle data was not updated after a store reset in week 4.
- 22% of customers who used coupons in store asked for coupons online. The coupon module was deferred.
- Alcohol handover refusals logged: 14. Two incidents of 17-year-old staff attempting handover (blocked by the system).
- The app's substitution chat was barely used by staff (0.3 chats per order).
- Service desk tickets: 212. Top category: handheld battery (41%).
