**MEMO: Compliance considerations for Click & Collect**
From: Janet Brooks, Chief Compliance & Privacy Officer

1. **Privacy.** Online accounts will collect personal data (name, phone, email, order history). This needs a privacy notice, explicit consent for marketing, and data retention of no more than 24 months for order history unless the customer opts in. Personalized offers based on purchase history require **opt-in** consent.
2. **Payments.** Card data must never touch GreenBasket servers. Use a hosted payment page or tokenization to keep PCI-DSS scope minimal.
3. **Food safety (state regulations).** Chilled items must be held at 5°C or below and frozen at −18°C or below from pick to handover. Temperature must be logged at least every 4 hours, and logs kept for 12 months. Hot food is out of scope.
4. **Alcohol.** 29 stores sell alcohol. Age must be verified with photo ID **at handover** by a staff member aged 18 or over, and refusal must be logged. In State C, alcohol cannot be handed over between 11 pm and 7 am. The customer's ID cannot be stored.
5. **Accessibility.** Digital channels must meet WCAG 2.1 AA (corporate policy since 2023).
6. **Union agreement (with HR).** New or changed duties for store staff need 45 days' consultation in States A and B.

*"I don't need to be in every meeting, but I need to see anything touching these six areas before approval."*
