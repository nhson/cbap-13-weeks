# Interview Transcript: Rosa Delgado, Store Manager, Riverton Central
*Conducted by: previous BA contractor (Harvest), Nov 2024. Unreviewed.*

**Q:** How do phone orders work today?
**Rosa:** The desk takes them on the SO-12 form, someone picks them in the afternoon, the chilled goes in the back cooler, and the customer pays at the desk till when they come in.

**Q:** How long does picking take?
**Rosa:** About 15 minutes for a normal order.

**Q:** What would make online pickup work?
**Rosa:** A dedicated picker in the morning. Not at the Saturday peak, that's chaos. And a staging area. The back cooler is too small.

**Q:** Stock accuracy?
**Rosa:** Mostly fine. Dry goods are good. Fresh is a guess.

**Q:** Alcohol?
**Rosa:** People would love it. We'd need someone over 18 at the pickup desk. Most of the evening staff are students, some are 17.

**Q:** Anything else?
**Rosa:** Don't let head office design this alone again. Talk to Kim. Kim knows.
