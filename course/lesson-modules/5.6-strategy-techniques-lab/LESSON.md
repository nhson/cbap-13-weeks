## Part 1: The toolkit

Share the link(s) with the student.

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- BMC 9 blocks: key partnerships, key activities, key resources, value proposition, customer relationships, channels, customer segments, cost structure, revenue streams.
- Capabilities describe WHAT the business does (not how or who). Capability maps show performance gaps and value or risk hot spots.
- Balanced scorecard: Learning and Growth, Business Process, Customer, Financial. Mixes leading and lagging indicators.
- Benchmarking compares with best practice or competitors. Market analysis studies customers, competitors and the market to find opportunities.
- Organizational modelling: functional, market-oriented and matrix structures; roles; interfaces; informal lines of influence.
