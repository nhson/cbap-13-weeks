## Part 1: The 4-step method

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- 1) Read the **last sentence** first: what is asked (task? technique? output? next step? FIRST/BEST/NOT?).
- 2) Identify the **context**: which KA and task is happening, which perspective (agile? BI?), and which state the information is in (unconfirmed? verified?).
- 3) **Eliminate**: out-of-role actions (BA approves alone, PM duties), wrong state labels, real-life-but-not-BABOK answers, absolute words (always/never).
- 4) Choose the answer that follows the task's purpose, involves the right stakeholders, and serves **value**.
- Pacing: 120 questions / 210 min. Checkpoints: Q40 at about 65 min, Q80 at about 135 min, Q120 at about 195 min, leaving 15 min to review flags.

## Part 2: Classic distractor patterns

ACTION: Re-read [Commonly Confused Concepts](CBAP%20Knowledge%20Graph/08%20Exam%20Lens/Commonly%20Confused%20Concepts.md) so every name you use is BABOK-exact. Share the link(s) with the student.

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- Right concept, wrong state ("validated" vs "verified").
- Right action, wrong owner (BA decides priorities or approves changes).
- Right action, wrong timing (escalate before trying collaboration; build before assessing).
- Real-world shortcut vs BABOK practice (skip confirmation because the sponsor is in a hurry).
- Plan vs perform confusion (3.3 plans prioritization; 5.3 performs it).
