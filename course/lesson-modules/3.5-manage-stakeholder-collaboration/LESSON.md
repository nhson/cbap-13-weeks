## Part 1: Manage Stakeholder Collaboration

ACTION: Re-read [Manage Stakeholder Collaboration](CBAP%20Knowledge%20Graph/03%20Tasks/Manage%20Stakeholder%20Collaboration.md) so every name you use is BABOK-exact. Share the link(s) with the student.

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- Purpose (in our words): Keep stakeholders engaged and working together towards the shared goal for the whole initiative.
- Elements: Gain Agreement on Commitments (time and resources), Monitor Stakeholder Engagement (participation, performance, risks such as diverted attention or delays), Collaboration (open communication, sharing, trust).
- Inputs: Stakeholder Engagement Approach, BA Performance Assessment. Guidelines: BA Approach, Business Objectives, Future State Description, Recommended Actions, Risk Analysis Results. Output: Stakeholder Engagement.
- Techniques: collaborative games, lessons learned, risk analysis and management, and stakeholder list, map or personas.

STOP: Marcus keeps cancelling. What does the BABOK say the BA should do first?

USER: Monitor engagement, understand the cause, re-secure commitment

ACTION: Give feedback. A strong answer includes: monitor engagement, identify the risk, understand the reason (e.g. Friday scheduling, funding concern) and gain agreement on commitments. Escalate to the sponsor only if that fails.

**Exam trap to call out:** Escalating to the sponsor as the *first* step is usually the wrong answer.
