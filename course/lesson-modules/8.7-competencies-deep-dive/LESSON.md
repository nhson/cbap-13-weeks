## Part 1: Behaviour → competency

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- Go group by group, giving one GreenBasket behaviour per competency, e.g. "reframed the delivery debate using the full survey data" = decision making / conceptual thinking; "kept every commitment to Kim" = trustworthiness / personal accountability; "facilitated workshop #2 neutrally" = facilitation; "negotiated central picker funding" = negotiation and conflict resolution; "taught stores to read the dashboard" = teaching.
- Watch out: ethics (fairness, transparency, confidentiality) vs trustworthiness (consistency, honesty over time).
