## Part 1: Underlying competencies

ACTION: Re-read [Underlying Competencies](CBAP%20Knowledge%20Graph/06%20Underlying%20Competencies/Underlying%20Competencies.md) so every name you use is BABOK-exact. Share the link(s) with the student.

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- Six groups: Analytical Thinking and Problem Solving (creative, decision making, learning, problem solving, systems, conceptual, visual thinking), Behavioural Characteristics (ethics, personal accountability, trustworthiness, organization and time management, adaptability), Business Knowledge (business acumen, industry, organization, solution, methodology), Communication Skills (verbal, non-verbal, written, listening), Interaction Skills (facilitation, leadership and influencing, teamwork, negotiation and conflict resolution, teaching), Tools and Technology (office productivity, BA tools, communication tools).
- Each competency has a purpose, a definition and effectiveness measures.
- Systems thinking = the whole and how the parts interact. Conceptual thinking = fitting details into the bigger picture and connecting seemingly unrelated information.

STOP: Rosa tells you about a workaround in her store, and you immediately see how it will affect pick accuracy, customer trust and store labour budgets. Which competency is that?

USER: Systems thinking

ACTION: Give feedback. A strong answer includes: systems thinking: understanding how changing one component affects the whole system.

## Part 2: Perspectives

ACTION: Re-read [Perspectives](CBAP%20Knowledge%20Graph/07%20Perspectives/Perspectives.md) so every name you use is BABOK-exact. Share the link(s) with the student.

Teach these points conversationally (short, concrete, GreenBasket examples where natural):
- A perspective is a lens for BA work in a specific context: Agile, Business Intelligence, Information Technology, Business Architecture, Business Process Management.
- Each describes change scope, BA scope, methodologies, underlying competencies, and impact on KAs.
- Agile: continuous, just-in-time elaboration, backlog, short iterations. BI: data to insight. IT: impact on systems. BA (business architecture): enterprise models. BPM: process improvement.

STOP: GreenBasket's pilot team works in 2-week sprints from a backlog. Which perspective frames that, and how does it change *when* requirements are elaborated?

USER: Agile; just-in-time, when needed

ACTION: Give feedback. A strong answer includes: Agile perspective: requirements elaborated just in time, prioritized backlog, continuous planning.
