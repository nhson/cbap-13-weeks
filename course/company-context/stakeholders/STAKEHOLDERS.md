# Stakeholder Profiles

Use these profiles when you role-play elicitation and when you analyze stakeholders. The BABOK role is given in brackets. The lessons ask you to confirm or challenge it.

---

## Elena Marsh, COO [Sponsor]
- Launched the initiative and controls the budget ($3.2M approved, with a contingency request pending) and the scope.
- **Wants:** a visible result within 12 months, and no repeat of Harvest.
- **Style:** decisive, impatient with detail, wants one-page summaries and clear options.
- **Attitude:** champion. High power, high interest.
- **Hidden concern:** her credibility with the board is tied to this.

## David Chen, CFO [Sponsor-adjacent / decision maker on investment]
- Approves any spending above $500k and any change to the business case.
- **Wants:** payback within 3 years and a clear NPV.
- **Attitude:** sceptical after Harvest. High power, medium interest.

## Marcus Lee, Head of Store Operations [Domain SME, key influencer]
- Runs 42 stores and has 22 years with GreenBasket.
- **Wants:** no extra burden on store staff, and protection of store P&L.
- **Attitude:** **resistant.** Harvest ignored his teams and he "told them it would fail". High power, high interest. Can block adoption.
- **Communication:** prefers face-to-face and distrusts slide decks.

## Rosa Delgado, Store Manager, Riverton Central [Domain SME / End user representative]
- Best-run store in the chain and a likely pilot store. Knows every workaround.
- **Wants:** a process that works during the Saturday peak, and enough staff.
- **Attitude:** curious but cautious. Medium power, high interest. Informal influencer among store managers.

## Kim Tran, Store Associate / picker, Riverton Central [End user]
- 4 years' experience; did the Harvest trial picks in 2024.
- **Knows:** why picking was slow (items not where the system said, no substitutions guidance, handheld scanners dying).
- **Attitude:** open, and will speak honestly if it feels safe. Low power, high interest.

## Anil Kapoor, Enterprise Architect [Implementation SME]
- Knows StockPoint, TillMaster and every integration pain.
- **Wants:** a clean integration architecture and no custom spaghetti.
- **Attitude:** supportive but warns about inventory data quality. Medium power.

## Helen Kowalski, CIO [Implementation SME / approver for technology decisions]
- Must approve the solution architecture and vendor selection. Worries about cybersecurity and PCI scope.

## Janet Brooks, Chief Compliance & Privacy Officer [Regulator (internal)]
- Enforces privacy law, PCI-DSS, food-safety cold chain rules and alcohol sale rules.
- **Attitude:** neutral and precise. Can stop a launch. High power, low-to-medium interest until something touches her domains.

## External regulators [Regulator]
- State food safety agency (cold chain, temperature logging), state liquor authorities (age verification), and the payment card industry (PCI-DSS via the acquirer).

## Tom Okafor, Project Manager [Project Manager]
- Owns schedule, budget and resources. Wants a stable scope early and fears scope creep.

## Priyanka Rao, QA Lead [Tester]
- Wants testable requirements with acceptance criteria. Reviews requirements for quality.

## Dev Patel, IT Service Desk Manager [Operational Support]
- Will support the solution after go-live. Wants runbooks, monitoring, and store-friendly error messages.

## Lily Park, Head of Digital Marketing [Domain SME]
- Wants flashy features: recipes, personalized offers, a same-day delivery "later". High interest, medium power. Adds scope.

## Ben Adeyemi, Category Management Lead [Domain SME]
- Owns product master data (images, allergens, sizes). Under-resourced.

## Owen Fitzgerald, Supply Chain Director [Domain SME]
- Owns replenishment and distribution centres. Cares about inventory accuracy and cold chain.

## Nadia Kowal, HR Director [Domain SME]
- Handles union relations. Any change to store roles or duties needs a union consultation in 2 states (**45-day notice**).

## Customers [Customer]
- **Persona "Busy Parent Sam"**: two kids, orders weekly, values reliability over price, hates substitutions without asking.
- **Persona "Retired Margaret"**: loyal in-store shopper, wary of apps, wants phone support.
- **Persona "Young Professional Jay"**: compares competitors, expects real-time availability and a pickup slot within 2 hours.

## Vendors [Supplier]
- **ShelfWise**: SaaS click-and-collect platform, fast to deploy, subscription pricing, limited customization.
- **CodeHarbor**: custom development agency (the same agency that built Harvest).
- **OmniGrocer Suite**: hybrid packaged platform with configurable modules and an integration layer.
