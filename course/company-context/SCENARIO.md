# The Scenario: GreenBasket "Click & Collect"

## Your role
You have just joined **GreenBasket Markets** as a **Senior Business Analyst**. You report to the Director of the Transformation Office and have been assigned to the company's most visible initiative of the year.

## The situation
GreenBasket is a regional grocery chain with 42 stores. In two years it has lost about 4 points of market share to competitors that offer online ordering with store pickup or delivery. GreenBasket offers neither.

The board wants a **"Click & Collect"** capability: customers order online and pick up at a store. The COO is sponsoring it, and the board has given the executive team **12 months** to show results.

There is history. A previous attempt, **Project Harvest** (2024), spent **$1.8M** and was cancelled before launch. People are wary, stores are sceptical, and the files it left behind are a mess.

## The arc (it follows your 13-week study plan)
| Study phase | What happens at GreenBasket |
|---|---|
| Foundations (wk 1–2) | You read the inherited files and learn who's who. |
| BA Planning & Monitoring (wk 3) | You plan how business analysis will work on this initiative. |
| Elicitation & Collaboration (wk 4) | You interview, observe, and run workshops with stores, IT and customers. |
| Requirements Life Cycle Mgmt (wk 5) | You trace, prioritize, handle a disruptive change request, and get approvals. |
| Strategy Analysis (wk 6) | You define current state, future state, risks, and the change strategy (business case). |
| RADD (wk 7–8) | You model, verify, validate and architect requirements, then recommend a vendor option. |
| Solution Evaluation (wk 9) | A 6-store pilot has run. You measure it, find the limitations, and recommend actions. |
| Consolidation & exam (wk 10–13) | You review everything as an examiner would. |

## Where things live
- `company-context/`: facts about GreenBasket and the people in it
- `case-files/`: the documents you inherit or receive along the way. They are deliberately messy, contradictory and incomplete, like real life.
- `workspace/`: the BA work products **you** create in the lessons
- `CBAP Knowledge Graph/`: the BABOK knowledge base, one note per concept
- `progress/`: your lesson log, quiz scores and error log
- `exams/`: diagnostic and mock exams
