# GreenBasket Markets: Company Profile

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Founded | 1987, family-owned until 2015; now majority-owned by a private equity fund |
| Stores | 42 (31 full-size, 11 "GreenBasket Express" convenience) in 3 states |
| Annual revenue | $1.1B |
| Employees | ~6,800 (about 85% in stores); store staff are represented by a union in 2 of the 3 states |
| Loyalty program | "Basket Rewards", 410,000 active members, points-based, app plus card |
| Market share (home region) | 18.2% in 2023 → 14.1% now |

| Strategic goal (board) | "Protect and grow our customer base by making GreenBasket the most convenient neighbourhood grocer" |

## How the business runs today
- **Store operations** are decentralized. Store managers have a lot of autonomy over layout, staffing and local promotions.
- **Inventory** is tracked in *StockPoint*, an on-premise inventory system from 2011. It is updated by nightly batch from the POS. Cycle counts are done monthly. Measured **inventory record accuracy is 82%** (industry leaders are above 95%).
- **POS** is *TillMaster v9*, with a real-time transaction feed only to the loyalty system.
- **Product master data** (names, images, sizes, allergens) is kept in spreadsheets by the Category team. About 30% of SKUs have no image. Allergen data is incomplete.
- **E-commerce:** the website is a brochure site with store locator and weekly flyers. The Basket Rewards app shows points and digital coupons only.
- **Fresh & cold chain:** food safety regulation requires chilled items to stay at 5°C or below and frozen items at −18°C or below, with temperature logs.
- **Alcohol** is sold in 29 stores. It needs age verification at handover, and state rules differ.
- **Payments:** card processing through a PCI-DSS compliant provider. Online card payment has never been done.
