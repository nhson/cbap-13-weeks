# 6.1 Analyze Current State

> [!abstract] Purpose (in our words)
> Understand why the enterprise needs to change and what the change would affect, starting from how things work today.

3. **Capabilities and Processes**
4. **Technology and Infrastructure**

6. **Business Architecture**
