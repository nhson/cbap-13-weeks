> [!tip] Exam lens
> - Basis for prioritization: benefit, penalty, cost, risk, dependencies, time sensitivity, stability, regulatory or policy compliance.
> - Challenges: non-negotiable demands, unrealistic tradeoffs. Prioritization is ongoing, iterative.
> - Know techniques inside Prioritization: grouping (MoSCoW), ranking, time boxing/budgeting, negotiation.
