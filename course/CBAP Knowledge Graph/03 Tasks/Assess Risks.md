# 6.3 Assess Risks

> [!abstract] Purpose (in our words)
> Identify and weigh the uncertainties that could reduce value during the transition or in the future state, and recommend how to proceed.

**Knowledge Area:** [[Strategy Analysis]] (15% of exam) · **BABOK** p.120 · ← [[Define Future State|6.2 Define Future State]] · → [[Define Change Strategy|6.4 Define Change Strategy]]

## Elements
1. **Unknowns**
2. **Constraints, Assumptions, and Dependencies**
3. **Negative Impact to Value**
4. **Risk Tolerance**
5. **Recommendation**
