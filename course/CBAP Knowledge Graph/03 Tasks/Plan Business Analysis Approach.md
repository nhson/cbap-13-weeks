# 3.1 Plan Business Analysis Approach

> [!abstract] Purpose (in our words)
> Decide how business analysis will be carried out on this initiative: the overall method, its formality, the activities, their timing, and how the approach gets agreed.

**Knowledge Area:** [[Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring]] (14% of exam) · **BABOK** p.24 · ← — · → [[Plan Stakeholder Engagement|3.2 Plan Stakeholder Engagement]]

## Elements
1. **Planning Approach**
2. **Formality and Level of Detail of Business Analysis Deliverables**
3. **Business Analysis Activities**
4. **Timing of Business Analysis Work**
5. **Complexity and Risk**
6. **Acceptance**
