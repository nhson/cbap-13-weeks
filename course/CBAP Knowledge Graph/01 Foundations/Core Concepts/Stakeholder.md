# Stakeholder

> [!quote] Definition (in our words; see BABOK 2.1)
> Anyone (individual or group) connected to the change, the need or the solution.

Usually described by their interest in, impact from, and influence over the change. See the 11 generic [[Stakeholder Roles]].

**Ask:** *Who is involved or affected?*
