# Solution

> [!quote] Definition (in our words; see BABOK 2.1)
> A particular way of meeting one or more needs within a context.

A solution meets a need by solving a problem or letting stakeholders exploit an opportunity.

**Ask:** *What are we building or changing to meet the need?*
