# Value

> [!quote] Definition (in our words; see BABOK 2.1)
> How much something is worth, or how important or useful it is, to a stakeholder in a given context.

Can be tangible (directly measurable, often monetary) or intangible (measured indirectly, e.g. reputation, morale); potential or realized; absolute or relative. Losses, risks and costs are negative value.

**Ask:** *What does each stakeholder regard as valuable?*
