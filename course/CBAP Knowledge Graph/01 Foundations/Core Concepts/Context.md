# Context

> [!quote] Definition (in our words; see BABOK 2.1)
> The surrounding circumstances that shape the change, are shaped by it, and help explain it.

Everything relevant to the change within the environment: attitudes, culture, competitors, governments, infrastructure, processes, technology, weather…

**Ask:** *What surrounds the change and the solution?*
