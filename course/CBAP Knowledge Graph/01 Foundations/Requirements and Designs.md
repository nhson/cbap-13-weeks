# Requirements and Designs (BABOK 2.5)

- **Requirements** focus on the **need**; **designs** focus on the **solution**.
- Both are elicited, modelled and analyzed with the same techniques. Requirements lead to designs, and designs often surface further requirements. The cycle repeats until the need is met.
- Tasks like [[Trace Requirements]] or [[Specify and Model Requirements]] refer to requirements, but *include designs*.
- The BA keeps asking **"why?"** to turn a stakeholder's stated solution into the underlying need.

| Requirement | Design |
|---|---|
| View six months of sales data across units in one view | A sketch of a dashboard |
| Reduce time to pick and pack an order | Process model |
| Record and access a patient's history | Screen mock-up with data fields |
| Develop business strategy, goals and objectives | Business capability model |
| Provide information in English and French | Prototype with bilingual text |
