# Key Terms (BABOK 2.2)

## Business Analysis
The practice of making change happen in an enterprise by clarifying needs and proposing solutions that create value for stakeholders.

## Business Analysis Information
Any information, at any level of detail, that business analysis uses or produces, such as elicitation results, requirements, designs, solution options, solution scope and change strategy.

## Design
A usable representation of a *solution*. It shows how value could be realized if the solution were built.

## Enterprise
One or more organizations, together with the solutions they use, pursuing shared goals. Its boundary is drawn around the change, not around a legal entity.

## Organization
An autonomous group under a single management that works towards common goals on an ongoing basis, unlike a temporary project team.

## Plan
A proposal for achieving something: events, dependencies, sequence, schedule, results, resources and people.

## Requirement
A usable representation of a *need*. It focuses on the kind of value that fulfilling it could bring.

## Risk
How uncertainty affects the value of a change, a solution or the enterprise. It can be reduced, removed, avoided, shared, accepted, or (for opportunities) increased.

> [!tip] Exam lens
> **Requirement ↔ need**, **design ↔ solution**. BABOK widened "requirements" to "business analysis information" so that every input/output is covered by the tasks.
