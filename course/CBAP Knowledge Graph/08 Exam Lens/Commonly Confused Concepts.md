# Commonly Confused Concepts

Pairs that exam distractors exploit.

## [[Verify Requirements]] vs [[Validate Requirements]]
Verify = quality of the requirement itself (well-formed, testable, unambiguous) — "built right". Validate = requirement aligns with business need and delivers value — "right thing".

## [[Confirm Elicitation Results]] vs [[Verify Requirements]]
Confirm checks the BA *understood* stakeholders correctly (4.3). Verify checks requirement *quality* (7.2).

## [[Solution Limitation]] vs [[Enterprise Limitation]]
Solution limitation = inside the solution (8.3). Enterprise limitation = culture, structure, operations outside the solution (8.4).

## [[Business Requirements]] vs [[Business Objectives]]
Business requirements (6.1) state the need/why. Business objectives (6.2) are SMART, measurable targets for the future state.

## [[Change Strategy]] vs [[Solution Scope]]
Both are outputs of 6.4. Change strategy = HOW the enterprise transitions; solution scope = WHAT the solution includes.

## [[Potential Value]] vs [[Solution Recommendation]]
Potential value (6.2) = value expected from the future state. Solution recommendation (7.6) = the option chosen as most valuable.

## [[Acceptance and Evaluation Criteria]] vs [[Metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)]]
Criteria assess or accept a solution at a point in time; KPIs measure ongoing performance against goals.

## [[Business Analysis Performance Assessment]] vs [[Solution Performance Analysis]]
The first measures the BA work (3.5); the second measures the solution (8.2).

## [[Stakeholder Engagement Approach]] vs [[Stakeholder Engagement]]
Approach = the plan (3.2). Stakeholder Engagement = the outcome of ongoing collaboration (4.5).

## [[Data Flow Diagrams]] vs [[Process Modelling]]
DFD shows how DATA moves and is transformed (no sequence/decisions). Process models show sequence of activities and decisions.

## [[Business Rules Analysis]] vs [[Decision Modelling]]
Rules analysis captures individual rules (definitional vs behavioural). Decision modelling shows how rules combine into a repeatable decision.

## [[Focus Groups]] vs [[Workshops]]
Focus groups gather opinions/attitudes (qualitative, not decisions). Workshops produce decisions/agreement on a defined outcome.

## [[Concept Modelling]] vs [[Data Modelling]]
Concept model = business vocabulary, independent of implementation. Data model = entities, attributes, relationships toward design.

## [[Glossary]] vs [[Data Dictionary]]
Glossary defines business terms. Data dictionary defines data elements (type, values, composition).
